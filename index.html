About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at SSN School of Engineering, Shiv Nadar University Chennai. My research interests include fault-tolerant and approximate adder and multiplier designs, as well as machine learning and deep learning accelerators using reconfigurable computing. I am also interested in developing customized hardware solutions for application-specific requirements.

I received my Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Design and Manufacturing (IIITDM), Kancheepuram, Chennai, under the supervision of Dr. Noor Mahammad SK. My doctoral research focused on “Fault-Tolerant Adder and Multiplier Designs for Mission Critical Systems.”

Research Interests

  • Reconfigurable Computing and FPGA-based Systems
  • Fault-Tolerant and Reliable Arithmetic Circuits
  • Approximate Adders and Multipliers
  • Machine Learning and Deep Learning Accelerators
  • Hardware Acceleration for Image and Video Processing
